Lecture 2: Web Architecture: Tiers and Layers

Summary

This lecture explains tiers and layers and why separation of concerns is important. It compares one-tier, two-tier and three-tier architectures.

Topics Covered

  • Tiers vs layers
  • Separation of concerns
  • One-tier (standalone) architecture
  • Two-tier (client-server): advantages and limitations
  • Three-tier / N-tier architecture
  • Choosing: scalability, maintainability, cost
